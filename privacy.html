Privacy Policy

Letter Loomer respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Letter Loomer is designed to keep gameplay simple. The app may store local progress on your device, such as unlocked levels, stars, settings, and tutorial completion state. This information is used only to support the gameplay experience on your device.

Analytics and Advertising

The app may use third-party services for analytics, attribution, or advertising support. Those services may collect technical information such as device type, app version, language, coarse usage events, or advertising-related identifiers when permitted by platform rules and your device settings.

How Information Is Used

  • To save your gameplay progress and settings.
  • To improve game stability, usability, and content performance.
  • To support app operations, including optional analytics or advertising features.

Children's Privacy

If the app is used by children, we aim to limit data handling to what is necessary for core functionality and compliant service operation. Parents or guardians who have questions may contact us directly.

Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device unless you remove the app or clear the app data through your device settings. Third-party partners may retain technical data according to their own policies and legal requirements.
